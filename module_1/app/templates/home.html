Sean Bae

Sr. Lead Technical Artist

About me

Sean (Sungwoo) Bae is a Sr. Lead Technical Artist with nearly 20 years of experience in the AAA game industry, specializing in character technology, rigging, animation, and technical art tools. Originally from South Korea, Sean has built his career at the intersection of art, computer science, and interactive technology.

He has contributed to internationally recognized titles and franchises including God of War, Titanfall, Star Wars, Avatar, Marvel Strike Force, and multiple Call of Duty titles, working with studios such as Activision Blizzard/Treyarch, EA/Respawn, Sony Interactive Entertainment, Riot Games, and FOXNEXT.

His professional interests include AI-assisted content creation, procedural rigging, animation systems, and digital production tools. Sean is passionate about connecting academic learning with real-world game development and sharing practical industry knowledge with the next generation of artists, technical artists, and game developers.
